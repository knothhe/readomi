A little room for thought

June 12 · 4 min read

Good tools make space for thought.

好的工具，为思考留出空间。

阅读不只是接收信息，也是在建立自己的理解。

Reading is more than taking in information; it is building your own understanding.

Keep the useful parts. Leave room for a new idea.

留下有用的部分，也为新的想法留一点空间。

Good ideas take time to settle.
